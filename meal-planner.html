Before you dive into The Menu, we want to say something. We designed The Menu ourselves, trained the AI behind it, and have personally tested its outputs. We've built in as many safeguards, filters, and double-checkers as we can, and we're confident it's a genuinely good guide. What it is not is a substitute for a registered nutritionist, especially if you have any ongoing health concerns, a history of disordered eating, or severe gastrointestinal issues. It's rare, but this programme can make mistakes, and we would never want to put any of you at risk. So please, if you have any specific concerns or health issues, book a private consultation with Nesse directly, or see a registered dietitian, rather than relying on The Menu alone.
