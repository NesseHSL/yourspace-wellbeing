Restore

For new mums finding their way back to movement

A gentle, expert-led programme designed to help you recover, rebuild, and return to the studio, safely, progressively, and at exactly your pace.

Register your interest See the programme

A note before you begin: Every postnatal body is different. We recommend getting clearance from your GP or midwife before starting any exercise programme after birth, typically at your 6-week check, or 8–12 weeks following a C-section. If you experience any pain, heaviness, or leaking during exercise, please stop and seek guidance from a women's health physiotherapist.

01

Pelvic Floor

Awareness, activation, and progressive strengthening of the pelvic floor, the foundation of every movement that follows.

02

Deep Core

Gentle rehabilitation of the transverse abdominis, safe for diastasis recti, essential for everything else.

03

Posture & Alignment

Addressing the postural patterns that pregnancy and feeding create, especially in the upper back, neck, and hips.

04

Hip & Glute Strength

Rebuilding the strength and stability you need to move well, progressively, without overloading a recovering body.

05

Full Body Strength

Progressive return to functional movement, the kind that supports life with a baby and prepares you for the studio.

06

Confidence

The quiet but important outcome nobody talks about, feeling at home in your body again, ready for whatever comes next.
