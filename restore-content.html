Before you begin

Welcome to Restore

This programme is here to support you as you find your way back to movement after birth, gently, and entirely at your own pace. There's no schedule to follow and nothing to unlock. All four videos are yours from today.

Restore is not a replacement for care from your GP or a women's health physiotherapist. If you haven't yet had your postnatal check, please get clearance from your GP or midwife before starting, and if anything below applies to you, please see a doctor or physio rather than continuing.

Stop and see a doctor or physio if you experience any of the following:

  • Persistent abdominal bulging or doming
  • Pelvic heaviness or pressure
  • Any leaking, from your bladder or bowel
  • Pain during intimacy
  • Worsening back pain
  • Bleeding beyond 8 weeks postpartum

If any of these apply to you, please pause this programme and see your GP or a women's health physiotherapist before continuing.
