The honest bit

This isn't just a workout app.

YourSpace is wellbeing, in its truest sense. Movement led by real experts, the same instructors behind HerSpace London, for the days you can't get to the studio. Nutrition and meal plans that actually make sense for your life. And when your favourite podcast tells you something that sounds a bit too good to be true, a way to actually check it against real science, not vibes.

Being well is bigger than a 45 minute class.

We built this because wellbeing was never really about the workout. It's about eating well, moving well, and not falling for nonsense. And once a year, it means putting it all together in Tuscany, at our Wine & Wellness Retreat. YourSpace is all of it.
