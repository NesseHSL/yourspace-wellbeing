1. About the Health Claim Checker and who operates it

The Health Claim Checker is a web-based evidence evaluation tool that assesses health claims against publicly available scientific literature. It is a feature of YourSpace Wellbeing, operated by HerSpace London, a company registered in England and Wales. It was previously published as a standalone product under the name CITE.

The Health Claim Checker uses the Anthropic API for evidence analysis and Tavily for live research retrieval. Claims you submit are processed by both services. The tool is designed to support public health literacy by making evidence evaluation accessible to non-specialists.

2. What it is, and what it is not

The Health Claim Checker evaluates discrete health claims using AI-powered analysis and live evidence retrieval. It returns a structured verdict indicating whether a claim is supported, inconclusive, or unsupported based on publicly accessible scientific literature at the time of query. Outputs include a summary verdict (always free) and, for paying users, a detailed analysis with evidence links, nuance, and contextual commentary.

Outputs are generated autonomously by AI and are not edited, filtered, or influenced by HerSpace London, its founder, or any commercial partner. No individual, brand, or organisation, including HerSpace London, has the ability to alter, suppress, or direct verdicts. We do not accept payment or other consideration in exchange for favourable verdicts.

Important limitations:

  • This tool does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations
  • Verdicts reflect the balance of publicly accessible evidence at the time of query, scientific understanding evolves
  • It performs best on discrete, testable claims. It is less suited to highly contextual clinical questions involving individual patient factors
  • It does not have access to unpublished, proprietary, or paywalled research
  • AI models can make errors. Outputs should be treated as a well-informed starting point, not a definitive determination

Not medical advice. Nothing produced by the Health Claim Checker constitutes medical advice. Do not make health, treatment, or medication decisions based solely on its outputs. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for personal medical guidance.
